Your privacy

  • Your conversation lives in this tab only. It is erased when you end the session.
  • The camera is off unless you turn it on. When it's on, your expression is read on your device, and only a short word like "worried" is passed to Ellis. If the on-device reader can't load (for example in the Claude preview), one still frame goes to Claude with each message instead. Ellis never records video or stores frames.
  • Your words (and camera frames, if the camera is on) are sent to Claude so Ellis can reply. Your browser's speech recognition may also process audio on its maker's servers. Type instead, and keep the camera off, if you'd rather avoid that.
  • Only your style choices, like "slower pace", are remembered on this device.
  • Ellis is an AI companion, not a licensed professional.
